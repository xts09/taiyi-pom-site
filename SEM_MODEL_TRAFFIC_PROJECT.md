# SEM Model-Intent Acquisition Project

## Purpose

Use selected POM grades to capture relevant material-selection demand in English-language European SEM first. Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan are secondary markets and remain on hold for third-party model-term activity until local legal review.

## Public-content rule

- Do not publish a broad competitor-model-to-Taiyi-model table.
- Do not say or imply: equivalent, 100% replacement, same as, original-grade equivalent, official alternative, or no-test replacement.
- Public pages present an independent Taiyi grade, its own source-backed data, application fit, document route, and sample/evaluation path.
- Mentioning third-party brands/models on public pages or in ad copy requires jurisdiction-specific legal approval. No competitor logos, packaging, images, or affiliation cues.
- A disclaimer can clarify context but does not remove trademark, advertising, or unfair-competition risk.

## SEM operating rule

- Create one ad group and one highly relevant landing page per selected Taiyi grade.
- Segment ordinary material/performance/application terms from third-party model-intent terms; use separate budgets.
- For Europe, a third-party model term may be tested as a backend keyword only after legal pre-screen. Ads and landing pages must clearly identify Taiyi and must not use the third-party name/model in public copy unless approved.
- Never treat purchasing a keyword as zero-risk. Review platform policy, jurisdiction, complaint history, and actual ad/landing-page presentation.
- Build and maintain negative-keyword lists for manuals, official distributor/agent requests, complaints, jobs, free downloads, research, and customers who require the original brand.

## Landing-page standard

- One core search intent per page; SEM test pages and durable SEO pages are not automatically the same deliverable.
- Use real material category, grade, properties/test methods, process direction, applications, colors/packaging/customization, document route, sample request, and technical contact.
- State that the grade is a candidate for evaluation; final fit depends on the part, tooling, process, end-use requirements, and customer testing.
- Do not copy competitor specifications or publish unsupported comparisons.

## Evidence and inquiry workflow

- Keep a technical evidence pack per grade: TDS, SDS, testing conditions/standards, sample results, available REACH/RoHS evidence, and application records.
- Current commercial baseline: the selected grades are stocked, samples can be arranged, and customization is available. Confirm the specific sample dispatch time and customization scope for each inquiry.
- REACH and RoHS must not be presented as automatically available for every grade. State that supporting documents can be requested and are confirmed or applied for according to the grade and project requirement.
- EGB25 uses supplied table data and is marked `data-only` because its formal TDS is provided through the Request TDS workflow rather than a repository-hosted PDF. The formal TDS is ready for external customer use. Verify the listed `Suggested Max Moisture: 5%` processing value against the controlled TDS.
- Capture current material, application, processing, key targets, color, annual volume, required certifications, and current problem in every inquiry.
- Sales and technical teams review together; send samples and support molding trials before any replacement commitment.

## Test gates

Before launch, define the target values for valid clicks, document downloads/sample requests, qualified-inquiry share, acceptable cost per qualified inquiry, sales acceptance, technical sample feasibility, and zero material confusion/complaints.

Test one sample page first with a limited budget for 2-4 weeks. Expand only after the pre-set gates are met. Pause related ads/pages immediately if a rights holder complains; preserve records and refer the matter to counsel.
